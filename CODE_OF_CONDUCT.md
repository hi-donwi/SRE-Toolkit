# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Critique the code,
not the person.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, publishing anyone's private
information, sexualised language or imagery, and sustained disruption of
discussion.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, and anywhere someone is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report anything that needs attention to <hi.donwi@gmail.com>. Reports stay
private.

I will look into it and respond. Depending on what happened, that may mean
editing or removing a comment, or blocking an account. I would rather have a
small, civil project than a large, unpleasant one.
